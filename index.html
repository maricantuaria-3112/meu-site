How can we create a new employee benefits category to attract, retain, and better support our workforce?

Our vision is to create a new category of employee financial benefits by giving workers seamless, on-demand access to their earned wages — whenever they need it.

Product vision & strategic value

This product empowers employees with greater financial flexibility, reducing dependence on high-interest loans and helping them better manage unexpected expenses — with no impact on the company's cash flow, and fast, seamless implementation.

Redesign to reduce friction

After the product launch, we identified the need for a redesign: users experienced uncertainty and hesitation when requesting a new salary advance, which led to an increased demand for support.

Problem understanding & design approach

We applied the Touch Level Model to evaluate the reading time required for each screen and identify opportunities to reduce cognitive load. Based on these insights, we simplified the experience by reducing reading time per screen, while intentionally increasing the number of steps — a step-by-step flow designed so users make only one decision per screen before moving forward, improving comprehension and confidence throughout the process.
